# Publication Checklist

- [ ] Confirm publication rights for the project name, branding, and concept visuals.
- [ ] Keep a visible statement that all product images are UI concepts, not actual screenshots.
- [ ] Review names, phone numbers, addresses, IDs, prices, receipt data, and timestamps in every concept image.
- [ ] Keep source code, databases, backups, keys, tokens, certificates, and logs private.
- [ ] Do not claim production readiness, customers, revenue, time savings, waste reduction, uptime, or throughput without evidence.
- [ ] Do not write “653 passing tests.”
- [ ] Do not claim an integrated card processor / gateway.
- [ ] Do not claim independent offline POS selling or host failover.
- [ ] Re-run verification before changing audit metrics.
